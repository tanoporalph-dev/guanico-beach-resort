Our Story · Since 2014

A different kind of escape.

Guánico began in 2014 as Surf Camp Guanico, a small backpacker hotel for travelers drawn to the waves and natural beauty of Panama's Pacific coast.

In April 2024, William Sanderson and the new ownership began transforming the property into a welcoming boutique beach resort, renovating rooms with private bathrooms, expanding the restaurant and introducing wellness facilities and adventure equipment.

Our philosophy is simple: escape crowded tourist destinations, experience the local way of life and enjoy nature without giving up comfort. We believe in genuine hospitality, supporting our community and helping every guest create their own adventure.

Before You Arrive

A few things to know.

Planning your escape to Playa Guánico? Here are answers to some common questions.

Contact Us
Where is Guánico Beach Resort located?

We are located in Playa Guánico, in the district of Tonosí, Los Santos province, on Panama's Pacific-facing Azuero Peninsula. Our remote beachfront location is ideal for travelers looking to escape crowded destinations.

Is breakfast included?

Yes. Breakfast is included in advertised room rates. The applicable 10% hotel tax is additional. Apartment and group package inclusions should be confirmed when booking.

Is the resort suitable for families?

Yes. Guánico is a relaxed, family-friendly boutique resort with comfortable accommodation, dining and activities for different interests. Our self-catering apartments accommodate up to four guests each.

Can beginners learn to surf?

Guests can enquire about surf coaching and the Free Demo Surfboard Program. Suitable sessions depend on experience, ocean conditions and instructor availability.

Can we book the entire resort?

Private group retreats for up to 20 guests can be arranged subject to availability. Groups staying five to seven days are particularly well suited to our intimate resort setting. Contact us for a tailored proposal.

How long should we stay?

We recommend five to ten days to enjoy the beach, explore the area and experience the local lifestyle. Longer stays are welcome, and extended-stay discounts may be available.

Is there a restaurant at the resort?

Yes. Palmeras restaurant offers international cuisine and wood-fired cooking, while the Guánico Tiki Bar provides a relaxed setting for drinks and good company.

How do I confirm my reservation?

Submit your preferred dates and accommodation through our inquiry form. The resort will confirm availability, rates and booking terms. A 15% deposit is required according to the confirmed reservation instructions.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 3:00 PM and check-out is at 12:00 PM. Early check-in and late check-out requests are subject to availability and approval.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellation and refund conditions will be provided with your reservation offer. Please review the confirmed terms before paying your deposit.
