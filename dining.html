Our Story · Since 2014

A different kind of escape.

Guánico began in 2014 as Surf Camp Guanico, a small backpacker hotel for travelers drawn to the waves and natural beauty of Panama's Pacific coast.

In April 2024, William Sanderson and the new ownership began transforming the property into a welcoming boutique beach resort, renovating rooms with private bathrooms, expanding the restaurant and introducing wellness facilities and adventure equipment.

Our philosophy is simple: escape crowded tourist destinations, experience the local way of life and enjoy nature without giving up comfort. We believe in genuine hospitality, supporting our community and helping every guest create their own adventure.

Before You Arrive

A few things to know.

Planning your dining experience or beachfront escape to Playa Guánico? Here are answers to some common questions.

Contact Us
Where is Palmeras Restaurant located?

Palmeras Restaurant and Guanico Tiki Bar are located at Guánico Beach Resort in Playa Guánico, Tonosí, Los Santos, on Panama's Pacific-facing Azuero Peninsula.

Can I dine without staying at the resort?

Contact us to arrange a restaurant visit if you are not staying overnight. Day visitors and dining inquiries are welcome, subject to availability.

What kind of food does Palmeras serve?

Palmeras focuses on international cuisine, wood-fired flavors, Pacific seafood, steaks and plant-based options. Ask for the current available menu.

Can I reserve a sunset dinner on the beach?

Yes, you can enquire about sunset dinners and beachfront seating. Arrangements depend on weather, tides, availability and confirmation from the restaurant.

Does dining include saltwater pool access?

Pool access for dining visitors is subject to resort arrangements and applicable day-visit terms. Contact us before your visit to confirm access and any charges.

Are vegan and vegetarian options available?

Ask about our available plant-based selections and vegan bowls. Please communicate allergies and dietary requirements before your visit so the restaurant can confirm suitable options.

What is the difference between Palmeras and The Break?

The Break reference menu focuses on sandwiches, smoothie bowls, smoothies and coffee. Palmeras offers a broader beachfront dining experience featuring wood-fired meals, seafood, steaks and the Guanico Tiki Bar.

Can you host private celebrations and group dinners?

We welcome inquiries for family gatherings, birthdays, group meals and private retreats. Catering, buffet meals and tailored dining arrangements can be discussed in advance.

Is breakfast included with resort accommodation?

Breakfast is included in advertised room rates, with the applicable 10% hotel tax charged separately. Apartment and group package inclusions should be confirmed when booking.

How can I reserve a table?

Use the table inquiry form or contact us directly through WhatsApp at +507 6772-5404. Our restaurant will confirm available times and arrangements.
